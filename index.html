FAQ

Common questions

Do you build fences in Round Rock?

Yes. Fence Crafters is a Round Rock fence company that installs and repairs fences across North Austin, Round Rock, Pflugerville, Cedar Park, Leander and Georgetown.

Do you come out and give an estimate?

Yes. We offer a free site visit and estimate, plus the information you need to make an informed decision.

What types of fences do you install?

Wood, ornamental iron and chain link fences in 4, 6 and 8 foot heights.

Do you repair fences, or only build new ones?

Both. We look at your fence and recommend the most cost-effective option, whether that is a repair or a replacement.

Do you do commercial fencing?

Yes. We build commercial fences that secure property, equipment and inventory, including iron and chain link with privacy slats, barbwire or razor wire.

Do you stain fences?

Yes. We offer transparent, semi-transparent and solid stains.

What wood is best for a privacy fence?

Western red cedar for the pickets, with pressure-treated pine for the posts and rails because it is structural and lasts longer.

How do you install iron fences?

We attach panels with the manufacturer's bolt-on bracket systems instead of welding, so the powder coat and galvanization stay intact.

How much does a new fence cost?

It depends on the length, material and height, so we visit your site and give you an estimate before any work starts.
